## 📝 Plot Summary

> "Two broke girls. One dream. One cupcake business."

Max, a sarcastic waitress with a tough past, meets Caroline, a former billionaire who lost everything. Together, they dream of opening their own cupcake business in New York City, navigating the challenges of their contrasting backgrounds.

---

## 💡 Why It's a Must-Watch

- **Witty Banter:** Max's sarcasm paired with Caroline's optimism creates hilarious dynamics.  
- **Entrepreneurial Spirit:** Showcases the journey of starting a business from scratch.  
- **Diverse Characters:** A rich tapestry of personalities adds depth and humor.
